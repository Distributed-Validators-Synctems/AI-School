# How much does the educational course cost, and what does it include?

The educational course costs $700 and consists of 10 mentor-led sessions, with each session lasting 2 hours.
Total: $700 for 20 hours of training ($35 per hour).

[Detailed course description is here](https://github.com/Distributed-Validators-Synctems/AI-School/blob/master/README.md)

# Why do you charge for the course? All the information is available online!

Everything we teach is publicly available, just like with English.
You can learn English on your own: all dictionaries, all grammar rules, and even free video courses for learning English are available online. Just search for "free English course."

Why do people still go to English tutors and pay $30 per hour?
It is the same with AI School.

If you want to and can complete the training on your own using information from the internet, then you do not need to join AI School.

If you want to get all the fundamentals and the full foundation as quickly as possible, in just 20 hours (3 weeks), then you pay for the educational course at AI School and pay the instructor for spending their time teaching you.

# What does the mentor charge money for?

You pay for the mentor's time. The mentor will explain how and what to download, where and what to click, where to get API keys, where to paste them, what to write to AI, and what instructions to give.

This person will need to show you what they already know and have patience when you are confused and do not understand simple things that are obvious to the mentor.

The mentor will not tell you that you are stupid and understand nothing. Instead, they will say: "No problem, we will figure everything out now. Yes, click the Sign Up button. No, not Log In, Sign Up. Yes, correct, now go to the Settings tab. In the upper-right corner. The right, not the left. Yes, click here. Choose the API Keys tab here."

That is what you pay for: the mentor's patience and the ability to avoid feeling stupid.

You know that you paid for the right to be confused, click the wrong place, and not hear anything bad from anyone, because you are a student and you have the right to know nothing about AI.
And if you did not understand the first time, you paid for the right to ask the same question a second time, a third time, and as many times as needed until you understand, while the mentor spends a lot of time explaining basic things to you.

# Why do I need AI School if I can learn everything on my own?

AI School is for people who cannot learn AI on their own and need help from a mentor.

For those who can learn independently, AI School is not needed. You can look at what our [Educational Course](https://github.com/Distributed-Validators-Synctems/AI-School/blob/master/README.md) consists of and find information about each session on your own.

Self-study will simply take you more than 20 hours.

# How will AI School help me find a job? How will I be able to earn money from the knowledge I gain at AI School?

We do not teach you how to earn money. We teach you how to use AI tools, how to launch teams of AI agents, and how to make them do something for you or develop something for you.

We teach you how to become a solopreneur (the owner of your own company), where all your employees are AI agents and complete your tasks 24 hours a day.
How do people earn money with English? And who will hire them because they know English? We have no idea. Knowing English simply helps with everything.

Who will hire you because you know AI tools and can launch teams of AI agents? We have no idea. This knowledge will simply help you with everything, just like English.

How will you earn money if you create your own company? It all depends on what your company does and how you build your marketing.

More about this in [Sessions 3 and 9](https://github.com/Distributed-Validators-Synctems/AI-School/blob/master/README.md)

# When do the sessions take place?

After the introductory session, you will join your cohort's private chat, where we will vote on the days and times for the sessions.

Usually, the sessions are on Mon-Wed-Fri at 15:00 UTC (17:00 CET / 18:00 KYIV / 18:00 MSK).

But if the group wants, the days may be Mon-Thu-Fri or Mon-Tue-Thu. The start time can also be earlier or later. Everything is based on a general vote by the students. Mentors will adapt to the students' choice.

# What happens if I miss a session? Will session recordings be available?

All sessions are recorded, and students get access to video recordings of all sessions, as well as text versions of all sessions, cheat sheets, links, and detailed guides on installing the required tools and what needs to be done.

If you miss a session, you will need to complete the session at home and show the result to the mentor.

If you have problems during a session, you will need to message the mentor in chat and arrange an extra call outside class.

You will share your screen, and the mentor will tell you where and what to click so that you can complete the session.

A session is considered complete if you have the required result after the session.
